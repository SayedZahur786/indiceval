Changes and attribution

HumanEval's first 50 tasks receive new Hinglish instructions while retaining signatures, helper code, canonical solutions and tests; original MIT notices remain. GSM8K-Hi questions and solutions are retained, with a numeric-answer format instruction and robust parsing of the final-answer marker. XCOPA Tamil premises, choices and labels are retained with a numbered response wrapper. XQuAD-IN Bengali contexts, questions and answers are retained with a short-answer instruction; only span-verifiable items are selected. XQuAD-IN adaptations remain CC BY-SA 4.0.

Consent boundary

No new participants were recruited and no private messages were collected. Upstream annotator consent paperwork is not available in these releases. If your launch policy requires that paperwork for every source, this release does not meet that gate. Source licensing and consent are recorded separately rather than conflated.

Contamination marker

The upstream canary is preserved in the dataset manifest. Published tasks and answers are downloadable for auditing. Do not treat public availability as evidence of contamination-free evaluation.

Report an issue

Use the task's source link and hash when reporting an answer, translation or rights issue in the repository. Known limitations and corrections are versioned publicly.
